# Product Guidelines: AI Tool Directory

## Visual Identity & Design Language
- **Aesthetic:** High-tech "SaaS" dark mode, following the existing project theme.
- **Color Palette:** Deep backgrounds (`#100F1B`), purple primary accents (`hsl(262 83% 58%)`), and subtle border gradients.
- **UI Components:** Glassmorphism, interactive spotlight effects, and Bento-style grids for information density.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (Satoshi/Switzer) with monospace accents for technical data.

## Content & Voice
- **Tone:** Objective and Analytical. The content should remain neutral, comparative, and focused on providing unbiased facts and figures.
- **Terminology:** Glossary-Assisted. Use standard technical AI terms (e.g., Context Window, Latency) but provide hoverable tooltips or contextual definitions for clarity.

## Information Architecture
- **Tool Overviews:** Outcome-Focused. Every tool summary must answer: "What can I build with this?" and "What specific problem does it solve best?"
- **Visual Comparisons:** Interactive & Explorable. Charts and graphs must be dynamic, allowing users to toggle metrics (e.g., Cost vs. Speed) and filter data points based on their specific needs.

## Brand Principles
- **Unbiased Authority:** Data must be the primary driver of all rankings.
- **Accessibility through Clarity:** Technical depth should never come at the cost of readability.
- **Actionable Insight:** Users should leave a tool page knowing exactly how it fits into their workflow.
